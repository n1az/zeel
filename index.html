Quick Guide

Word-by-Word

Enable Word-by-Word in the navbar to see each Arabic word's English translation inline.
🖥 Desktop: hover a word to highlight it.
📱 Mobile: each word is tappable.

Research Mode

With Word-by-Word ON, click (or double-tap on mobile) any Arabic word to open a research panel showing how many times it appears in the Quran — with links to every verse.

Transliteration

Enable Transliteration to see a phonetic romanised reading guide beneath each verse — helpful for learning Arabic pronunciation.

Translation

Switch between English and Bengali translations with multiple scholar versions available in the top navbar.

Navigate Surahs

Use the Surah dropdown in the top bar to jump to any of the 114 Surahs instantly. On mobile, tap ☰ to open the settings drawer.

Dark / Light Mode

Toggle between light and dark themes using the switch on the far right of the navbar — always visible, even on mobile.

About Zeel Quran

Zeel is a modern Quran reading application with an unapologetic neo-brutalist design, featuring English and Bengali translations along with English transliteration and word-by-word research tools to help deepen your understanding of the Quran.
